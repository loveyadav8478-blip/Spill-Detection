Attribution Engine Mechanics

This operations deck executes a modular monolith oil spill pipeline. Uploading the satellite imagery and mask maps triggers coordinate calculations, which are cross-referenced with vessel tracks to attribute liability.

1 SAR Imagery & Spill Mask

Ingests a georeferenced synthetic aperture radar (SAR) file and its spill segmentation mask to isolate pixel count, perimeter, area size, and PCA shape eccentricity.

2 Drift Hindcast Trajectory

Uses localized windage coefficients, current vectors, and weather APIs to compute the historical backward drift path to determine the location and time window of origin.

3 AIS Tracking Correlations

Filters nearby vessel transponders and calculates matching scores (proximity, heading course anomaly, speed deviation, transponder blackout gaps) to rank suspects.
